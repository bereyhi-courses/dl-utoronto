description: We study sequence data and their processing by NNs. We see that due to temporal connection, we cannot process them separately. At the same time, the one-shot processing is not feasible. We hence need to develop a sequence model. We study the most basic design for sequence processing, i.e., a recurrent NN. We see that finding the right choice of state in these architectures is rather crucial.
